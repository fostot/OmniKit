# Holiday companion body artwork provenance

This second holiday collection replaces the v1 sheets from release 0.1.0.0015. All eight PNGs are unchanged raw outputs from the built-in image generation tool, with real transparency. V1 source assets and metadata remain preserved locally; their published release is unchanged. Everyday source images are unchanged.

Each companion has nine separately named holiday transformations with distinct materials and silhouettes: All Saints' Wake, Starlight, Heavensturn, Valentione, Little Ladies, Hatching Tide, Make It Rain, Moonfire Faire and The Rising. Optional head accessories remain separate. Eight complete poses per look retain connected bodies, stable source scale, measured head anchors, crown grips and blink sources. Orbit remains ringless for runtime rings; Wink retains one eye.

Source pixels were not painted, erased or resampled. Measurement and native preview tools produced diagnostic crops only. Every full sheet was inspected at 96/192 pixels. The runtime frame renderer passed 1,152 body-bound checks, 576 crown checks and 144 Wink closure checks. These are offscreen rendering checks, not live-game validation.

PNG authenticity metadata was reviewed as public tool/format/software provenance. No game screenshots, profiles, account content, credentials or private paths were supplied. The prompts below preserve submitted wording; line endings and trailing whitespace are normalized.
